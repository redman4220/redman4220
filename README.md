# Jeffery Dingess

I am a dispatch operations professional with more than nine years of experience coordinating emergency response, triaging incidents under pressure, and keeping accurate records when the stakes are high. As the solo builder of Assistant Hub, a private-first crypto intelligence platform, I designed security controls such as encrypted key storage, read-only wallet access, and a tamper-evident audit log, because I believe people deserve privacy, transparency, and control over their own data. I am now earning my Google Cybersecurity Certificate to bring that same calm, detail-oriented approach to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of an organization's assets and the people it serves.
